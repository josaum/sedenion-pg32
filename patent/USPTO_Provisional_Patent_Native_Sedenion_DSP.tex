\section*{BACKGROUND OF THE INVENTION}
\textbf{[0003]} All conventional multi-antenna digital signal processing (DSP), phased-array radar, and Controlled Reception Pattern Antenna (CRPA) receivers formulate received wavefields as vectors in an $N$-dimensional complex Hilbert space $\mathbb{C}^N$ governed by associative, commutative complex scalar multiplication. While foundational, the $\mathbb{C}^N$ linear-algebraic paradigm suffers from four fundamental physical and computational limitations in contested electromagnetic environments:

\textbf{[0004] 1. Pre-Beamformer ADC Saturation, LNA Compression, and Co-Linear Blindness:} In conventional Digital Beamforming (DBF), raw RF/IF signals from $N$ antenna elements are amplified by Low-Noise Amplifiers (LNAs) and digitized by Analog-to-Digital Converters (ADCs) prior to spatial null-steering. Under high-power Electronic Attack (EA) where the Jammer-to-Signal Ratio ($\mathrm{JSR}$) reaches $+60\text{ dB}$ to $+80\text{ dB}$, the jammer waveform saturates the LNAs and exceeds the full-scale dynamic range of 12-bit to 16-bit ADCs, causing severe non-linear clipping that destroys the buried desired signal before digital weights can be applied. Furthermore, when a jammer arrives co-linearly ($\Delta\theta = 0.0^\circ$) with a target satellite, any spatial-only $\mathbb{C}^N$ beamformer steering a null toward the jammer simultaneously extinguishes the satellite signal (degrading the GPS L1 C/A code-phase Cram\'er--Rao Lower Bound from meters to $>126\text{ km}$).

\textbf{[0005] 2. $\mathcal{O}(N^3)$ Sample-Matrix Inversion and Sample Starvation:} Adaptive beamformers such as Minimum Variance Distortionless Response (MVDR / Capon) and Loaded Sample Matrix Inversion (LSMI) must accumulate $K \ge 2N$ spatial snapshots to estimate a covariance matrix $\hat{\mathbf{R}}_{xx} = \frac{1}{K}\sum_{k=1}^K \mathbf{x}[k]\mathbf{x}^H[k]$ and compute its inverse $\hat{\mathbf{R}}_{xx}^{-1}$ at $\mathcal{O}(N^3)$ computational complexity. During high-G platform maneuvers, rapid roll rates, or agile blinking jammers, the interference Angle of Arrival (AoA) slews faster than the $K$-snapshot integration window, smearing $\hat{\mathbf{R}}_{xx}$ and degrading null depth by $25\text{--}40\text{ dB}$.

\textbf{[0006] 3. Rank-1 Covariance Collapse Under Coherent Repeater Spoofing:} When a Meaconing, Intrusion, Jamming, and Interference (MIJI) repeater spoofer or specular multipath reflection arrives coherently with the authentic signal (sharing the same baseband waveform $s(t)$ at zero or near-zero delay and Doppler offset), the received snapshot is $r(t) = s(t)(\mathbf{u}_{\mathrm{auth}} + \alpha \mathbf{u}_{\mathrm{spoof}})$. Across any number of snapshots $K$, the signal subspace of $\hat{\mathbf{R}}_{xx}$ collapses to \textbf{Rank 1}. Consequently, classical eigenvalue-ratio ($\lambda_2/\lambda_1$), MUSIC, ESPRIT, and AIC/MDL rank detectors are mathematically blind to zero-lag coherent spoofing.

\textbf{[0007] 4. Vulnerability of Scalar Spread-Spectrum Waveforms to Radiometric Interception:} Conventional BPSK/QPSK Direct-Sequence Spread Spectrum (DSSS) waveforms modulate a 1D complex phase and remain vulnerable to cyclostationary feature detection and cross-correlation interception.
