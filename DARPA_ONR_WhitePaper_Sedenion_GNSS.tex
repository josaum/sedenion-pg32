\textbf{2. How is it done today, and what are the limits of current practice?}\\
Terrestrial GPS L1 C/A signals arrive roughly $20\,\mathrm{dB}$ below thermal noise ($\approx -130\,\mathrm{dBm}$). Current multi-antenna anti-jamming and anti-spoofing architectures face three structural bottlenecks:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Ill-Conditioned $\mathcal{O}(d^3)$ Matrix Inversion \& Finite-Sample Sidelobe Bleed:} Classical Space-Time Adaptive Processing (STAP) and Minimum Variance Distortionless Response (MVDR / Capon) Sample Matrix Inversion (SMI) must invert a $d \times d$ sample covariance matrix $\hat{\bm{R}}_{xx}^{-1}$ in $\mathcal{O}(d^3)$ operations. At $\mathrm{JSR} = +80\,\mathrm{dB}$, the condition number $\kappa(\hat{\bm{R}}_{xx}) \ge 10^6$ amplifies FP32 round-off, while sub-degree ($0.5^\circ$) jammer drift and diagonal loading cap practical null depth at $47.9\,\mathrm{dB}$, leaving the GPS PRN correlation peak buried ($P_d = 8.2\%$).
    \item \textbf{The Hurwitz Division-Algebra Obstruction in Polarimetric Arrays:} Hypercomplex array processors based on complex ($\C^8$), quaternion ($\Ha^4$), or octonion ($\Oc^2$) division algebras obey Hurwitz's norm identity $\|u \cdot v\|_2 = \|u\|_2 \|v\|_2$. They are algebraically prohibited from annihilating a non-zero interferer $u \neq 0$ via single-channel multiplication ($u \cdot v = 0$ is impossible).
    \item \textbf{RTK Carrier-Phase Cycle Slips:} Re-estimating unconstrained MVDR weights when a jammer activates rotates the complex passband phase, causing Phase-Locked Loop (PLL) cycle slips ($>15^\circ$) that break centimeter-level navigation.
\end{itemize}
